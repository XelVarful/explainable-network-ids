# Literature Review

## Purpose

Review research on machine learning for network intrusion detection
and explainable AI, with an emphasis on SHAP.

Target: 35–40 scientific publications, predominantly published
within the last five years.

## Review Process

1. Check the title and abstract for relevance.
2. Read the methodology, results and limitations.
3. Record the dataset, models, evaluation protocol and XAI methods.
4. Summarize findings and their relevance to our research.
5. Link detailed notes for each reviewed paper.

Status definitions:
- Identified: potentially relevant; detailed review pending.
- In progress: full-text review underway.
- Reviewed: notes completed and checked against the source.
